1. The BIS standards

For each standard, first answer: is the BIS standard available? If Yes, upload the PDF - the app reads the year, the name and the Scope from it. If No (a paid standard that was not bought), the app takes the year and name from the BIS website, and that standard is left out of the Scope slide and of the standards zip. If none of the standards is available, the Scope slide is removed from About BIS and from its Index. For every standard, type the date of implementation and the number of licensees in India.
